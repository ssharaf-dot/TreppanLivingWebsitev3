05.1 — Smart Home Automation

A home that responds.

Treppan Living homes are designed to be intelligent, intuitive, and responsive. Lighting, climate, curtains, access, and comfort settings can be integrated into smart-home systems that make everyday living more seamless.

01

Lighting Control

Dimmable lighting, scene presets, motion-based lighting, app control, night modes, energy-efficient logic. Light that follows life.

02

Climate Control

Smart thermostats, room-level temperature control, scheduling, occupancy-based settings, HVAC integration, app-based control.

03

Curtain & Blind Automation

Motorised curtains, scene integration, time-based scheduling, sunlight-based automation, app control, privacy presets.

04

Scene-Based Living

Morning, work, relax, dinner, movie, sleep, away, vacation. One touch — the right atmosphere.

05

Voice / App Control

Voice assistants and the resident app, integrated to give control without complication.

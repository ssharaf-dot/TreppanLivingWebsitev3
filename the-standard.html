01 — Philosophy

A home should do more.

Where others sell the address, we engineer the experience — invisibly, continuously, and by design.

A home should do more than shelter you. It should help you breathe better, drink better, sleep better, recover better, live easier, use less, waste less, feel safer, feel calmer, feel connected.

Traditional residential projects sell lifestyle through location, views, finishes, and amenities. But the future of home will be defined by something deeper — the invisible systems that shape how people feel, live, and behave every single day.

Treppan Living is built on the belief that the future of home is not only beautiful — it is intelligent, healthy, responsible, and alive.

02 — Vision

Where we're going.

A future in which homes actively support health, simplify daily life, and make sustainable living the default — not the exception, not the upgrade, not the marketing claim.

A future in which every residence is part of a living standard — measurable, accountable, and continuously improving over the life of the building.

03 — Mission

What we're building.

A coherent platform that integrates physical wellness systems, smart-home technology, sustainability infrastructure, and resident experience into one living standard — adopted by developers, operated by buildings, and felt by residents every day.

The Treppan Living Standard is not a label. It is a set of enforceable, auditable specifications that translate promise into performance.

06 — Technical Standards

The invisible, made legible.

Wellness and sustainability only mean something when they are measured. Treppan Living surfaces the factors that shape how a home actually feels and performs — continuously, and in plain sight.

Air
CO2

Carbon Dioxide

Ventilation effectiveness — the difference between an alert mind and a heavy room.

Air
VOC

Volatile Compounds

Off-gassing from materials, cleaners, and furnishings — tracked, not assumed.

Air
PM2.5

Fine Particulate

The microscopic pollutants the lungs cannot filter on their own.

Air
RH

Humidity

The single biggest driver of perceived comfort — held in a healthy range.

Air
T°

Temperature

Climate control tuned to the human body, not the thermostat default.

Air
hPa

Pressure

Barometric awareness for sensitive sleepers and high-performance recovery.

Water
pH

Water pH

Balance verified at the tap — not at the source, not on the brochure.

Water
TDS

Dissolved Solids

A direct readout of water purity — the simplest honest signal there is.

Water
L/d

Consumption

Daily water use, surfaced gently — awareness without alarm.

Water
Leak

Leak Status

Continuous monitoring at fixtures — quiet protection, all hours.

Energy
kWh

Energy Use

Consumption visible per circuit — the home's pulse, made readable.

Impact
CO2e

Footprint

The cumulative environmental cost of daily life — counted, not estimated.

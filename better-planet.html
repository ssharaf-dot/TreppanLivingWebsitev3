06.1 — Energy Intelligence

Smarter energy. Lighter footprint.

Treppan Living uses technology to help buildings and residents understand how energy is consumed and where efficiency can be improved. Through smart meters, automation, optimised systems, and energy dashboards, energy use becomes visible, manageable, and more responsible.

01

Smart Energy Monitoring

Apartment-level tracking, common-area tracking, usage dashboards, consumption trends, alerts for abnormal usage, efficiency recommendations.

02

Common-Area Efficiency

Lighting, lifts, ventilation, shared systems optimised for low-impact operation.

03

HVAC Optimisation

Occupancy-aware control, predictive scheduling, ongoing tuning of building systems.

04

Solar / Renewable

On-site renewable integration where appropriate to the project and site.

05

Energy Dashboards

Live data for residents and building teams — informing behaviour, not just reporting it.

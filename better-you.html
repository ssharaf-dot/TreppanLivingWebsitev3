Two things you do without thinking

Breathing and drinking — the two things a home owes you.

Eight dimensions of air and water, engineered into the building. Measured constantly, optimised in the background, surfaced in the app when you want to look.

01

Indoor Air Quality, Live

CO2, VOCs, PM2.5, temperature, humidity, pressure — measured constantly, shown in your app, optimised in the background.

02

Multi-Stage Water Purification

Sediment filtration, activated carbon, reverse osmosis, UV sterilisation. Beats WHO drinking standards. Every tap, every time.

03

NASA-grade Filtration

Hospital-class HEPA paired with activated carbon. Particulates, allergens, odours, VOCs — down to 0.1 microns.

04

Hydrogenated & Mineralised

Remineralised post-purification with calcium and magnesium. Hydrogenation for taste and benefit. Water you actually want to drink.

05

Humidity, Held in Range

Maintained 40–60%, the sweet spot for respiratory health, skin, and the materials in your home.

06

Vitamin-C Showers

Filters that neutralise chlorine and chloramine. Optional aromatherapy infusion. Showers become a small daily ritual.

07

Demand-Controlled Ventilation

Fresh air adjusts to occupancy, CO2, and outdoor conditions. Energy-recovery ventilators stop comfort from costing.

08

Leak Detection & Cut-off

Sensors at every critical point. Automatic shut-off in seconds. The kind of safety net you only notice once it’s saved you.
